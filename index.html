How to play

At the top of the page is the start of a chain of words.

You can add a word to the chain if the start of the word overlaps with the end of the last word by 2 or more.

For example, if your starting word is "education", you could follow it with "onset", "ionic" or "educational".

The aim of the game is to unlock every pair of letters in the target word at the bottom of the screen by adding words to the chain that end in those pairs of letters. They can be unlocked in any order.

For example, if your target word is "book", a winning chain must contain a word that ends in "bo" and a word that ends in "ok". e.g. "placebo" and "took".

If that all sounds complicated, don't worry, it's easier than it sounds. You'll get the hang of it fast.

At the end you get a score based on how many words are in your chain and how many times you undid progress. The lower your score, the better.

There are bonus point deductions available for using the minimum possible number of words or unlocking the endings in the order they appear in the word.
